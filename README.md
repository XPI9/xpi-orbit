# XPI Orbit

The web browser from XPI Solutions. Search by typing or speaking, keep your bookmarks and history on your own computer, and turn on Vicky, the built-in assistant, whenever you want help finding or summarizing something.

## Download

Get the latest Windows installer from the [Releases page](../../releases/latest).

1. Download `XPI-Orbit-Setup` and double-click it.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. XPI Orbit opens when setup finishes. Look for the XPI Orbit icon on your desktop.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

## Good to know

- Voice search prepares itself the first time you use it. This takes a moment and needs an internet connection once.
- Vicky is optional. Switch her on or off under Settings (the gear icon).
- Bookmarks and history stay on your computer.
